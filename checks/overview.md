# Overview: checks

Scripts behind the classification of numbers in *The language of labels in physics* (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23871788).

The classification of numbers is checked by `ga_number_catalog_check` (NC1–NC12), `ga_compact_integers_check` (CI1–CI8), `ga_attractor_check` (AT1–AT5) and `ga_label_link_check` (LK1–LK4); the Tully–Fisher value by `de_btfr_running_check` (BR1–BR3) and `de_btfr_rt_check` (RT1–RT5), with SPARC data.

# paper11: checks

Scripts that compute the statements of *Black holes in the label framework: collapse, streams, vacuum cores and the label count*
(doi:10.5281/zenodo.24021764); all print `PASS`.

- `ga_blackhole_labels_check` (BH1–BH6): Oppenheimer–Snyder collapse of label dust; shell crossing in the marginally bound LTB solution;
  two four-label vacua joined only through a wall; shell crossing as multi-streaming; the lattice step from the entropy coefficient;
  Hawking temperature, label count and emission of label matter
- `ga_vacuum_q_check` (VQ2, VQ3): the stress and the constant of integration of the four-label vacuum
- `ga_rindler_eta_check` (RE1–RE8): the area coefficient of the entanglement entropy (as in paper8)
